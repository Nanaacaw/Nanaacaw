## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Data Scientist** and **AI Engineer** who enjoys taking ideas from raw data all the way to
products people can actually use. My work sits at the intersection of **machine learning**,
**large language models**, and **computer vision** — building systems that don't just predict, but
**understand, reason, and see**.

Lately I've been focused on **applied AI**: designing **RAG pipelines** that ground LLMs in real
knowledge, shipping **document-intelligence** tools that turn messy scans into structured data, and
training **vision models** for detection, segmentation, and recognition. I care about clean data,
reproducible experiments, and turning models into something that delivers real value.
